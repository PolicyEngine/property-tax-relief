# Property tax relief across America

A source-linked review and interactive comparison of property-tax credits, circuit breakers, and renter credits in all 50 US states and the District of Columbia.

## Scope

The comparison uses tax year 2025. Later changes are identified separately. Statewide credits and rebates are the calculation scope; exemptions, assessment limits, deductions, deferrals, and local programs provide context and are not interchangeable with credits. Every jurisdiction must appear in the inventory, including those without an identified statewide credit.

Every numerical household result must come from the PolicyEngine model for that exact scenario. Model coverage and legal coverage are audited separately. Missing implementations must not appear as zero benefits. Public release requires complete national research, validated applicable calculations, and independent review.

## Work in progress

This repository is under active research and is not a filing tool or a completed national comparison. Primary sources, verification notes, calculation coverage, tests, and reproducible generation scripts will accompany the report and interactive.
